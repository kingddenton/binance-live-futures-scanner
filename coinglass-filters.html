Calculation notes

RSI uses Wilder's RSI, period 14, calculated from completed candles. Price change compares the close of the latest completed candle with the preceding completed candle of the same duration. Volume change compares the quote-volume sums of two adjacent equal-duration periods using completed 1-hour candles. For 24h volume, this means 24 completed hourly candles versus the preceding 24.

The 24h RSI and price-change options use Binance daily candles, which follow Binance's daily candle boundaries; they are not rolling 24-hour calculations. Binance rate limits or network errors can affect scanning speed.

Funding, open interest and liquidation filters are not implemented in this version.

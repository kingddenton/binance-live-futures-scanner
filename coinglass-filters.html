Supertrend + OI + RSI — scan settings

RSI qualification

Choose one or more timeframes. All selected RSI conditions must pass for the relevant direction. RSI is calculated from the current candle data returned by Binance.

Supertrend qualification

ATR(10), multiplier 3. All selected Supertrend timeframes must agree with the direction being tested. Bullish and bearish results are calculated separately.

Open interest (OI) qualification

OI change is calculated from Binance USD-M Futures open-interest history: (latest OI ÷ previous OI − 1) × 100. When OI qualification is enabled, unavailable OI data cannot qualify a pair.

Optional DEA confirmation

When enabled, bullish results require DEA at or above the bullish threshold; bearish results require DEA at or below the bearish threshold.

Display-only filters

These controls filter the visible results after qualification. They do not change the RSI, Supertrend, OI or DEA qualification logic.

Signal scan settings

RSI confirmation — select multiple timeframes

Every selected RSI timeframe must pass. Default bullish RSI is above 60; default bearish RSI is below 40. Leave a timeframe unchecked to ignore it.

Supertrend confirmation — select multiple timeframes

All selected Supertrend timeframes must agree with the signal direction. Mixed directions mean no signal. At least one RSI or Supertrend timeframe must be selected.

Optional DEA confirmation

When enabled, bullish signals require DEA at or above the bullish minimum; bearish signals require DEA at or below the bearish maximum.
